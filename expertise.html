Mandatory Forensic Energy Calculation Standards

NEVER calculate electrical energy consumption using SUM(active_power * dt) trapezoidal integration.

Show detail (5)Hide detail
  • Active Energy (total_active_energy) (kWh): Calculated strictly via MAX - MIN over target window to determine actual billing consumption.
  • Line-to-Neutral Voltage (voltage_l_n) (Volts (V)): Identifies voltage sags (<215V vs 235V nominal) and grid over-voltage events that stress inductive motor loads.
  • Current Draw (current) (Amperes (A)): Monitors phase loading; triggers automated high-current alarms when threshold exceeds 15A.
  • Grid Frequency (frequency) (Hertz (Hz)): Audits national grid stability (nominal 50.0 Hz); flags thermal generator trips during deviations.
  • Power Factor (power_factor) (Ratio (0.00 – 1.00)): Measures electrical phase displacement; diagnoses capacitor bank failures when PF dips below 0.80.
