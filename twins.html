Digital twins

A model of your plant that tells you what is wrong.

Each twin is built for one client's plant, on their data, and it stays theirs. That is why there are no public demos here. Below is what a twin does, how an engagement runs, and the platforms delivered so far.
Get a twin of your plant

01Why a twin

What a twin gives your operations team

  • Health per component

    Every reading judged against healthy physics at the same load and conditions, not a fixed threshold.

  • Faults blamed correctly

    Each fault lands on its own component with a reason an engineer would write, not a wall of alarms.

  • Energy lost, in kW

    What the plant draws against what a healthy plant would draw, so maintenance has a price tag.

  • Ready for agents

    The twin is a tool an AI agent can call: “what is wrong, and what does it cost?”

02Engagement

Digital Twin Pilot

A physics-informed live twin of one plant system, connected to your BMS or gateway data, with fault detection and energy accounting. From $40k, 4–8 weeks.
  1. Step 1

    Data audit and point mapping (BACnet, Modbus, MQTT)

  2. Step 2

    Physics model of the asset, calibrated on your data

  3. Step 3

    Live isometric twin with health and fault views

  4. Step 4

    Energy-loss report against a healthy baseline

03In production

Nine production twins

Operating 9 production-grade, physics-informed digital twins combining dynamic simulation with physical ClickHouse hardware telemetry.
  • Next.js 14+
  • SVG Vector Modeling
  • WebGL
  • Server-Sent Events
  • Physics Modeling
  • Docker

  • Metro Homes Distribution Microgrid

    300 residential households across 20 city blocks

    • SAIFI (System Average Interruption Frequency Index)
    • SAIDI (System Average Interruption Duration Index)
    • CAIDI

  • AI Factory & High-Density Data Center

    50 MW high-density computing facility housing 360 server racks (40 kW to 100 kW per rack for NVIDIA H100/B200 clusters)

    • Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE): 1.18
    • Cooling Loop Delta-T: 12.4 °C (Supply 32°C / Return 44.4°C)
    • Facility Load: 42.8 MW Compute / 7.7 MW Thermal Rejection

  • Industrial Cold Storage Facility

    4,940 Metric Ton (MT) multi-chamber industrial ammonia (NH3) refrigeration facility

    • Specific Power Consumption: kW/TR (Kilowatts per Ton of Refrigeration)
    • Chamber Holding Temp: -22.0 °C
    • Thermal Pull-Down Rate

  • Smart Village Feeder Microgrid

    Rural electrification grid covering 120 distributed agricultural and residential consumer nodes

    • Phase Imbalance % (R-Y-B phases)
    • Neutral Current Displacement
    • Technical Line Losses (I²R)

  • 33/11 kV Primary Distribution Substation

    Dual-transformer 2 × 10 MVA primary distribution substation feeding municipal districts

    • Busbar Voltage (33 kV / 11 kV)
    • MVA Loading per Transformer
    • OLTC Tap Position (1-17)

  • Commercial Office Tower VRF

    10-floor corporate office tower featuring 359 indoor VRF fan coil units and 20 rooftop outdoor condensing units

    • BEE Energy Index (kWh/m²/year)
    • Average Indoor Temperature (23.5 °C)
    • VRF Compressor Inverter Frequency (Hz)

  • Central Chiller Plant

    3 × 800 TR water-cooled centrifugal chillers with primary-secondary pumping loops and cooling towers

    • Plant Efficiency: 0.58 kW/TR
    • Chiller COP: 6.06
    • Cooling Tower Approach: 2.8 °C

  • Floor Zone Architectural VRF

    Single-floor detailed CAD architectural layout with room-level thermal load zones

    • Zone Airflow CFM
    • Room Temp vs Setpoint
    • Electronic Expansion Valve (EEV) Opening %

  • Microgrid Islanded Power

    Campus hybrid microgrid: 1.2 MW rooftop solar PV, 2.5 MWh Battery Energy Storage (BESS), 1.5 MVA diesel generator

    • State of Charge (SoC %)
    • Microgrid Grid-Tie Frequency (50.0 Hz)
    • Diesel Fuel Flow Rate (L/hr)
