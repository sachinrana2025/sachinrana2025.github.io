04Process

How an engagement runs

  1. Step 1

    Brief and call

    Your brief, then thirty minutes on the problem, constraints and what success looks like. No charge.

  2. Step 2

    Written proposal

    Scope, architecture direction, milestones and a fixed or capped price within a few days.

  3. Step 3

    Delivery

    Weekly reviews, working software in your repositories, and decisions documented as they are made.

  4. Step 4

    Handover

    Runbooks, training and full IP transfer, so your team owns what was built.
